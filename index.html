介系詞

介系詞要怎麼記？

從介系詞的意思開始，再看月份、日期和地點前面要用哪個字。

什麼是介系詞？

介系詞（preposition）是用來表示關係的詞。 它通常和後面的名詞、代名詞或動詞的 -ing 形式一起用，說明時間、地點、方向、方式等。

The new branch will open in May. 新分店將在五月開幕。in May 回答「什麼時候開幕」，in 就是介系詞。

時間｜時刻 at、哪天 on、大範圍 in

at 9 a.m.

鐘上的一點。也記 at noon、at midnight、at night。

on May 12

日曆上一格。星期、日期或特定某天的時段，如 on Monday morning。

in May

一段範圍。月份、年份、季節、一般早午晚，如 in 2026、in the morning。

月份怎麼考？ 只有月份用 in May，月份加日期就變成 on May 12。三個都出現可寫 at 9 a.m. on May 12。

直接記成整組

  • at night（晚上）、in the evening（傍晚或晚上）、on Friday night（星期五晚上）。
  • on the weekend 常見於美式英文，at the weekend 常見於英式英文。
  • next Monday、last May、this morning、every Friday、tomorrow 在這些時間片語前直接使用。
  • in two days 可以是「兩天後」（The shipment will arrive in two days.），也可表示完成動作用了兩天。within two days 強調「兩天之內」。

地點｜把地方看成點、面或範圍

at the entrance

定位點、集合處、活動地點。記 at the station、at work、at 25 King Street。

on the desk

表面、樓層、沿線。記 on the wall、on the second floor、on Main Street（常見於美式）。

in the office

在空間或區域裡。記 in the lobby、in Taipei、in Taiwan。

at the hotel
把飯店當成會面或活動地點。I'll meet you at the hotel.
in the hotel
特別強調人在建築物內。The café is in the hotel.
交通工具
位置記 on the bus / train / plane、in a car / taxi。問交通方式則用 by bus / train / car 或 on foot。
活動與場地
at a conference 在會議活動現場，in the conference room 在會議室內。

方向｜在那裡，還是移動過去？

to / toward(s) / from
walk to the station 以車站為目的地，walk toward the station 朝車站走，walk from the station 從車站出發。
in / into / out of
wait in the lobby 在裡面等，walk into the lobby 從外走進去，walk out of the lobby 從裡走出來。
on / onto / off
stand on the platform 在月台上，step onto the platform 踏上月台，step off the platform 離開月台表面。
across / through / along / past
across the street 橫越馬路，through the tunnel 穿過隧道內部，along the river 沿河，past the bank 經過銀行。

抵達與回家：把 arrive at the station、arrive in Taipei、go home、get home 當整組記。reach 可直接接地點，如 reach the station。

易混用法｜看句子要表達什麼

by / until / before
Submit the form by Friday. 最遲週五交。Stay here until Friday. 一直待到週五。Call before Friday. 週五以前打電話。
since / for / during
since May 從五月起，for three months 持續三個月，during the meeting 會議期間。接完整子句可用 while we were meeting。
on time / in time
start on time 按表定時間開始，arrive in time to catch the train 及時趕上火車。
at the end of / in the end
at the end of the month 在月底，in the end, we agreed 最後我們達成共識。
between / among
between the three companies 把三家公司視為可區分的對象，among the employees 指在員工群體之中。
above / over / below / under
above average 高於平均、over the bridge 越過橋、below zero 低於零、under the table 在桌子下方。部分空間語境可以互換。
by / with / via
written by Mia 由 Mia 寫，signed with a pen 用筆簽，sent via email 透過電子郵件寄出。
to / for
send the file to Amy Amy 是接收者，print a copy for Amy Amy 是受益者。for 也可表用途，如 a tool for checking invoices。
from / of
a message from the manager 來源是經理，a copy of the report 副本所對應的是報告。
because of / despite
because of the rain 因為下雨，despite the rain 儘管下雨。接完整子句可用 because it rained 或 although it rained。
like / as
work like a manager 做事像經理，work as a manager 以經理身分工作。

多益搭配｜單字和介系詞一起背

動詞
apply for a job 申請工作、apply to a company 向公司申請、depend on the results 取決於結果、participate in a workshop 參加工作坊、comply with the rules 遵守規則。
形容詞
responsible for the budget 負責預算、interested in the position 對職缺有興趣、familiar with the system 熟悉系統、eligible for a refund 符合退款資格、satisfied with the service 對服務滿意。
名詞
access to the portal 使用入口網站的權限、an increase in sales 銷售成長、a solution to the problem 問題的解法、a reason for the delay 延誤的原因。
商務片語
in charge of 負責、in accordance with 依照、on behalf of 代表、prior to 在……以前、with regard to 關於。

每天挑三組，用自己的工作或生活寫一句。做選擇題時，把空格前面的單字和後面的名詞一起看。

介系詞查表

英文的介系詞也包含許多多字片語，收錄範圍會隨文法分類而變。這裡收錄常見詞和商務搭配，每個詞條都有記法、常見片語和自編的中英對照例句。英文句子裡的粗體就是該詞條的介系詞。

沒有符合的詞條。可以清除關鍵字或換個類別。

aboard
在船、飛機或其他交通工具上，想成已登車或登船 aboard the ferry

All passengers must be aboard the ferry by 8 a.m.所有乘客都必須在上午八點前登上渡輪。

about
談主題時是「關於」，放在數字前也可表大約 a question about the invoice

I have a question about the delivery fee.我有個關於運費的問題。

above
位置或數值高於一條線 above the entrance / above average

A security camera is mounted above the entrance.入口上方裝了一台監視器。

according to
根據某人、文件或資料來源 according to the report

According to the latest report, online orders rose by 12 percent.根據最新報告，線上訂單增加了百分之十二。

across
從一邊到另一邊，也可指在對面 walk across the street

We walked across the street to the new café.我們穿過馬路，走到那間新開的咖啡廳。

after
排在時間或順序的後面 after lunch

I'll call the supplier after lunch.我午餐後會打電話給供應商。

against
靠著表面，或站在反對的一方 against the wall / against the proposal

The ladder was leaning against the wall.梯子斜靠在牆上。

ahead of
在前面，也可指早於預定時間 ahead of schedule

The maintenance team finished the repairs ahead of schedule.維修團隊提前完成了修繕。

along
跟著一條線或路徑 along the coast

Several restaurants are located along the waterfront.沿著水岸有好幾家餐廳。

alongside
在旁邊，或和某人一起工作 work alongside the design team

Our engineers worked alongside the design team on the new model.我們的工程師和設計團隊一起開發新機型。

amid / amidst
置身於環境或一連串情況中 amid rising costs

The company froze hiring amid rising costs.在成本攀升的情況下，公司暫停招募。

The new service launched amidst uncertainty about market demand.在市場需求尚未明朗時，這項新服務上線了。

among / amongst
把對象想成一群，在其中或分配給群體 among the participants

The organizers divided the remaining materials among the participants.主辦方把剩下的資料分給參加者。

The survey found strong support amongst employees for flexible hours.調查發現，員工普遍支持彈性工時。

apart from
可表示「除了……之外另有」或「扣除……」，依上下文判斷 apart from a small fee

Apart from one minor typo, the brochure is ready to print.除了一個小錯字，這份型錄已經可以付印。

around / round
繞著、在附近，也可指大約時間 around the building / around noon

The courier usually arrives around noon.快遞員通常在中午左右到。

We walked round the warehouse looking for the loading area.我們繞著倉庫走，找裝卸區。

as
以某種身分或用途來看 work as a translator

Maya joined the company as a sales assistant.Maya 進公司擔任業務助理。

as for
把話題轉到某件事上 as for the schedule

As for the venue, we've already booked the main hall.至於場地，我們已經訂好主會場了。

as of
指定某個生效或統計的時間點 as of June 1

As of July 1, all visitors will need a badge.從七月一日起，所有訪客都必須佩戴訪客證。

as per
依照某項要求或指示，常見於正式書面語 as per your request

As per your instructions, we sent the samples to your Taipei office.依照你的指示，我們已將樣品寄到你的台北辦公室。

as to
指出所談論的問題，常見於正式書面語 uncertainty as to the cause

There was some confusion as to the exact start date.大家對確切的開始日期還有些疑問。

as well as
在已有的一項之外再加一項 training as well as equipment

The package includes technical support as well as training.這套方案除了教育訓練，也包含技術支援。

aside from
除某事之外，可依語境表排除或補充 aside from the setup fee

Aside from the setup fee, there are no extra charges.除了設定費，沒有其他額外費用。

at
對準一個點，也可表目標或價格 at noon / at the entrance / at $50

The interview begins at 9 a.m.面試上午九點開始。

I'll meet you at the main entrance.我會在正門和你會合。

away from
與某處拉開距離 move away from the door

Keep the boxes away from the emergency exit.箱子請放在離緊急出口遠一點的地方。

barring
除非出現所說的例外 barring delays

Barring any last-minute changes, the store will open on Monday.除非臨時有變，這家店會在星期一開幕。

because of
把後面的名詞當成原因 because of heavy traffic

The driver arrived late because of heavy traffic.司機因為塞車而遲到。

before
在某時間點之前，也可指在某人面前 before noon / appear before the board

Please confirm your order before noon tomorrow.請在明天中午以前確認訂單。

behind
在後方，或進度落後 behind the building / behind schedule

You'll find the delivery entrance behind the building.你可以在大樓後方找到送貨入口。

below
位置或數值低於一條線 below the window / below zero

Sales fell below our quarterly target.銷售額低於這一季的目標。

beneath
在下方，語氣常較正式 beneath the floorboards

The technician found a damaged cable beneath the floorboards.技術人員在地板下面發現一條受損的電纜。

beside
就在旁邊 beside the window

I left the signed form beside your laptop.我把簽好的表格放在你的筆電旁邊。

besides
在已有的一項之外再加 besides the monthly fee

Besides the monthly fee, there's a one-time setup charge.除了月費，還要付一筆一次性的設定費。

between
在可分辨的對象或界線之間 between the two terminals

A shuttle runs between the two terminals every ten minutes.兩個航廈之間每十分鐘有一班接駁車。

beyond
超出某個範圍或界線 beyond our budget

The revised quote is beyond our budget.修改後的報價超出我們的預算。

but
在 everyone、nothing 等後可表示「除了」 everyone but Alex

Everyone but Daniel attended the briefing.除了 Daniel，大家都出席了簡報會議。

by
緊靠、由誰執行、用哪種方式，或最遲時間 by the door / by train / by Friday

Please send the signed contract by Friday.請最遲在星期五寄出簽好的合約。

We went to the trade show by train.我們搭火車去參加商展。

circa
用於大約年代，常見於歷史或正式書寫 circa 1900

This photograph was taken circa 1910.這張照片大約攝於 1910 年。

concerning
把後面的名詞當作討論主題 a notice concerning the merger

We received a notice concerning the upcoming merger.我們收到一份關於即將進行的合併案的通知。

considering
把某項因素納入判斷 considering the cost

Considering the cost of repairs, we decided to replace the printer.考量維修費用後，我們決定換一台印表機。

contrary to
與某個說法或預期相反 contrary to expectations

Contrary to our expectations, demand stayed strong after the sale.和我們預期的相反，促銷結束後需求仍然強勁。

depending on
結果跟著條件改變 depending on availability

Delivery takes three to five days, depending on your location.送貨需要三到五天，視你所在的地點而定。

despite
即使有某種阻礙，事情仍然發生 despite the delay

Despite the shipping delay, the team met its installation deadline.雖然出貨延誤，團隊仍在期限內完成安裝。

down
朝低處走，也可指沿著街道走 down the stairs / down the street

Go down the stairs and turn left at the bottom.沿著樓梯往下走，走到底再左轉。

due to
把後面的名詞當成原因 a delay due to snow

The evening flight was canceled due to heavy snow.晚上的航班因大雪而取消。

during
指出事件發生的那段期間 during the conference

Please keep your phone on silent during the presentation.簡報期間請將手機設為靜音。

except
把某人或某物從範圍中排除 every day except Sunday

The help desk is open every day except Sunday.客服櫃台除了星期日，每天都開放。

except for
整體描述成立，特別指出例外 empty except for one desk

The meeting room was empty except for a stack of brochures.會議室裡只有一疊型錄，其他東西都沒有。

excluding
計算或清單中排除某項 $80 excluding tax

The room costs $120 per night, excluding tax.房費每晚 120 美元，未含稅。

following
接在某事件之後，常見於正式書面語 following the interview

Following the interview, HR sent me a short survey.面試後，人資寄了一份簡短的問卷給我。

for
記成「持續多久」、「給誰」或「為了什麼」 for two weeks / for the client

We've rented this office for five years.我們租用這間辦公室已經五年了。

I ordered a vegetarian lunch for our guest.我幫客人訂了一份素食午餐。

from
記成來源或起點 from Taipei / from Monday to Friday

The samples arrived from our Osaka office this morning.樣品今天早上從大阪辦公室寄到了。

given
把事實或條件當成考量 given the current budget

Given the short deadline, we'll need another designer.考量到期限很緊，我們還需要一位設計師。

in
放進時間、空間或狀態的範圍 in May / in the office / in stock

The new branch will open in May.新分店將在五月開幕。

Your visitors are waiting in the lobby.你的訪客正在大廳裡等候。

in accordance with
按照規定或正式指示 in accordance with company policy

We store customer records in accordance with company policy.我們依照公司規定保存客戶資料。

in addition to
在原有項目上再加 in addition to shipping costs

In addition to training new staff, Nina manages the weekly schedule.除了訓練新進員工，Nina 也負責排定每週班表。

in case of
如果發生某種情況 in case of emergency

In case of a power outage, use the stairs.如果停電，請走樓梯。

in charge of
負責某事或管理某人 in charge of hiring

Leo is in charge of ordering office supplies.Leo 負責採購辦公用品。

in exchange for
用一項東西換另一項 in exchange for a discount

The supplier offered free delivery in exchange for a larger order.供應商願意免運，條件是我們增加訂購量。

in favor of
站在支持的一方 in favor of the proposal

Most of the committee voted in favor of the new schedule.委員會多數成員投票支持新時程。

in front of
在某物的前方 in front of the hotel

The airport shuttle stops in front of the hotel.機場接駁車停在飯店前面。

in light of
考量新資料或情況之後 in light of recent changes

In light of recent feedback, we've simplified the registration form.考量最近收到的意見，我們簡化了報名表。

in lieu of
正式用語，表示拿一項代替另一項 in lieu of payment

The hotel offered a voucher in lieu of a cash refund.飯店提供折抵券來代替現金退款。

in place of
頂替某人或某物的位置 in place of the original speaker

Elena gave the presentation in place of her manager.Elena 代替主管做簡報。

in response to
為了回應某項請求或事件 in response to your inquiry

We added a second session in response to high demand.為了因應踴躍的報名需求，我們加開了一場。

in spite of
即使有某種阻礙，事情仍然發生 in spite of the rain

The outdoor event went ahead in spite of the rain.儘管下雨，戶外活動仍照常舉行。

in terms of
從某個面向來說 in terms of cost

In terms of cost, the second option is better for us.就成本而言，第二個方案對我們比較有利。

including
把某項算進範圍 including delivery

The rental costs $90 a day, including insurance.租金一天 90 美元，包含保險。

inside / inside of
在邊界裡面，inside of 也可用於部分口語語境 inside the warehouse

The spare monitors are inside the storage room.備用螢幕放在儲藏室裡。

I left the signed form inside of the blue folder.我把簽好的表格留在藍色資料夾裡。

instead of
選另一項來替代原來那項 instead of printing the report

We emailed the agenda instead of printing it.我們用電子郵件寄送議程，省去列印。

into
從外到內，也可表變成另一種狀態 walk into the room / turn into ice

The staff moved the extra chairs into the conference room.工作人員把多餘的椅子搬進會議室。

like
和某人或某物相像 work like a professional

This material looks like leather.這種材料看起來像皮革。

minus
從總數中減去某一項 the total minus the deposit

The final payment is the total price minus your deposit.最後應付的金額是總價扣掉訂金。

near / near to
距離近。near 可直接接地點 near the station

There's a pharmacy near the station.車站附近有一間藥局。

Our hotel is quite near to the airport.我們的飯店離機場很近。

next to
緊鄰某物 next to the elevator

You'll find the sign-in sheet next to the coffee machine.你可以在咖啡機旁邊找到簽到表。

notwithstanding
正式用語，表示儘管 notwithstanding the objections

Notwithstanding the objections from several members, the board approved the plan.儘管有幾位成員反對，董事會仍批准了這項計畫。

of
把兩個名詞連起來，可表部分、所屬或內容 a copy of the invoice

Could you email me a copy of the invoice?你可以寄一份發票副本給我嗎？

off
離開表面或離開路線 get off the bus

Please get off the bus at the next stop.請在下一站下車。

on
日期是日曆的一格，地點常指表面或沿線 on Monday / on the wall / on Main Street

The training session is on Monday morning.訓練課程在星期一上午。

The meeting room is on the second floor.會議室在二樓。

There's a floor plan on the wall near the elevator.電梯附近的牆上有一張樓層平面圖。

on account of
把後面的事情當成原因 on account of bad weather

The concert was postponed on account of strong winds.演唱會因強風而延期。

on behalf of
代表某個人或團體 on behalf of our team

On behalf of the entire team, I'd like to thank you for your help.我謹代表整個團隊感謝你的協助。

on top of
放在頂上，或再加一項 on top of the cabinet

The spare keys are on top of the filing cabinet.備用鑰匙放在文件櫃頂上。

onto
移動到某個表面上 step onto the platform

The workers loaded the pallets onto the truck.工人把棧板裝上卡車。

opposite
在正對面 opposite the library

The new branch is opposite the public library.新分店在公共圖書館正對面。

out
少數用法可直接接方向或出口，美式英文也常見 out the door out the door

The courier hurried out the side door.快遞員匆匆從側門出去。

out of
從裡面出來，也可指用完或超出範圍 out of the office / out of stock

That model is currently out of stock.那個型號目前缺貨。

outside / outside of
在邊界外，也可指範圍之外 outside the station

A taxi is waiting outside the hotel.有輛計程車在飯店外等候。

The building is locked outside of business hours.辦公時間以外，大樓會上鎖。

over
在上方、越過、超過數字，或跨一段時間 over the bridge / over $100 / over the weekend

The shuttle crossed over the bridge just before sunset.接駁車在日落前不久開過那座橋。

We received over 200 applications this week.我們這週收到了超過兩百份申請。

owing to
表原因，常見於正式書面語 owing to staffing shortages

The library will close early owing to a staffing shortage.圖書館因人手不足而提早關門。

past
從旁邊經過，或過了某個時刻 walk past the bank / past midnight

Walk past the bank and turn right at the next corner.經過銀行後，在下一個轉角右轉。

pending
在等某事決定或完成的期間 pending approval

The purchase is on hold pending budget approval.採購案暫緩，等預算核准後再處理。

per
平均到每一單位，也可表依照 $20 per person

The workshop costs $35 per person.工作坊每人收費 35 美元。

plus
在原有數量上再加一項 $80 plus tax

The replacement part costs $40 plus shipping.替換零件要 40 美元，運費另計。

prior to
在某事件以前，語氣比 before 正式 prior to departure

Check your passport's expiration date prior to departure.出發前請確認護照的有效期限。

pursuant to
按照法律、合約或規定執行，常見於正式文件 pursuant to the agreement

Pursuant to the contract, the supplier must replace defective units within 30 days.根據合約，供應商必須在三十天內更換瑕疵品。

regarding
後面就是這段話談的主題 an email regarding your order

I received an email regarding my refund request.我收到一封關於退款申請的電子郵件。

regardless of
條件怎麼變都適用 regardless of age

All employees can join the course regardless of experience.無論有沒有相關經驗，所有員工都可以參加這堂課。

save for
正式用語，表示把某項排除 empty save for one chair

The parking lot was empty save for one delivery van.停車場裡除了一輛送貨廂型車，沒有其他車輛。

since
從過去的某個起點延續 since May

We've used the same supplier since May.我們從五月起就一直跟同一家供應商合作。

subject to
受某個條件限制，常見於公告 subject to availability

Late checkout is subject to room availability.能否延後退房視房間供應情況而定。

such as
在一般類別後面列例子 office supplies such as pens

You can buy basic office supplies such as pens and folders here.你可以在這裡買到筆和資料夾等基本辦公用品。

thanks to
指出原因，常有多虧某事的語氣 thanks to your help

We finished the move early thanks to the volunteers.多虧志工幫忙，我們提早完成搬遷。

than
放在比較級後面，引出比較的基準 larger than the old office

The new conference room is larger than the old one.新的會議室比舊的大。

through
穿過內部、藉由某方式。美式時間範圍也可含終點 through the tunnel / Monday through Friday

The delivery truck drove through the tunnel.送貨卡車開過了隧道。

The help desk is open Monday through Friday.客服櫃台從星期一到星期五都開放。

throughout
遍及整段時間或整個區域 throughout the year

Free Wi-Fi is available throughout the hotel.整間飯店都有免費 Wi-Fi。

till
持續到某個時間點，意思和 until 相同 till noon

The café is open till six.咖啡廳營業到六點。

to
朝目的地、交給接收者，也可標示範圍終點 to the airport / to Amy / from nine to five

Please send the invoice to our accounting department.請把發票寄給我們的會計部門。

We took a shuttle to the exhibition center.我們搭接駁車去展覽中心。

toward / towards
朝某方向，或表示對某事的態度 walk toward the exit

Follow the signs toward the main exit.沿著指標朝主要出口走。

The delivery driver walked towards the loading dock.送貨司機朝卸貨區走去。

under
在下方、低於數值、受管轄或處於某狀態 under the table / under $50 / under review

A spare box of cables is under the desk.桌子底下有一箱備用電線。

The application is still under review.申請仍在審查中。

underneath
在正下方或被蓋住的下面 underneath the carpet

The cleaner found a room key underneath the rug.清潔人員在地毯下面找到一把房間鑰匙。

unlike
把後面的對象拿來對比差異 unlike the old system

Unlike the old printer, this one can scan both sides of a page.這台和舊印表機不同，可以掃描紙張的兩面。

until
動作或狀態一路持續到某時 open until 6 p.m.

The reception desk will stay open until 8 p.m.接待櫃台會開放到晚上八點。

up
向上，或沿著某條路前進 up the stairs / up the road

We walked up the hill to the training center.我們走上山坡，到達訓練中心。

up to
最多到某上限，或由某人決定 up to ten guests / up to you

Each reservation can include up to six guests.每筆預訂最多可包含六位客人。

upon
較正式的 on；在 upon arrival 中指一抵達 upon arrival

Upon arrival, please check in at the front desk.抵達後，請到櫃台辦理報到。

versus
引出比較或對決的另一方 online sales versus in-store sales

The report compares the cost of rail versus air travel.這份報告比較搭火車和搭飛機的費用。

via
透過某管道，或經由某個中途點 via email / via Singapore

We'll send your confirmation via email.我們會以電子郵件寄送確認通知。

with
一起、有某特徵，或使用某工具 with a colleague / with a pen

Please sign the form with a black pen.請用黑筆簽署這份表格。

I reviewed the proposal with my manager.我和主管一起審閱了這份提案。

with a view to
以做某事為目標，接名詞或 V-ing with a view to expanding

The company rented a second warehouse with a view to expanding its operations.公司為了擴大營運，租了第二座倉庫。

with regard to
指出所談論的事項 with regard to the new policy

With regard to your order, the blue model is back in stock.關於你的訂單，藍色款已經補貨了。

with respect to
就某事項而言，常見於正式書面語 with respect to payment

With respect to payment, the full amount is due by the end of the month.付款方面，款項須於月底前全數付清。

within
在界線、期限或限制內 within two days / within budget

We'll respond within two business days.我們會在兩個工作天內回覆。

without
缺少某物，或在沒做某動作的情況下 without a receipt / without signing

You can't enter the warehouse without a visitor badge.進入倉庫必須佩戴訪客證。

worth
用來指出價值，或表示某事值得做 worth $100 / worth reading

This training guide is worth reading before your first shift.這份訓練指南值得在第一次上班前讀一讀。

小測驗｜選介系詞

先選一個，再展開看答案。

The workshop starts ___ June. （at / on / in）

in。只有月份，當成較大的時間範圍。

The workshop starts ___ June 12. （at / on / in）

on。有明確日期，當成日曆上一格。

The reception desk is ___ the second floor. （at / on / in）

on。樓層用 on。

Please meet me ___ the main entrance. （at / onto / in）

at。集合時，把大門當成定位點。

Please submit the form ___ Friday at the latest. （by / until / since）

by。at the latest 指最遲時間，交件可在期限前或期限當下完成。

The help desk will remain open ___ 6 p.m. （during / until / since）

until。開放狀態會持續到六點。

Eva has worked here ___ three years. （since / during / for）

for。三年是時長。若要寫起點，可用 since 2023。

The delivery truck went ___ the tunnel. （along / through / onto）

through。車子穿過隧道內部。

動詞句型

to 後面加原形還是 V-ing？

先認識「不定詞」和「介系詞」，再看兩種 to 後面各接什麼。

什麼是不定詞？快速判斷to + 原形to + V-ing易錯句型小測驗

什麼是不定詞？

不定詞（infinitive）是一種動詞形式，本身不隨主詞或時態變化。 有 to 的不定詞是 to + 動詞原形，例如 to learn。動詞原形就是字典裡的基本形式，例如 learn、work、be。

  • I want to learn English. 我想學英文。
  • She wants to learn English. 她想學英文。
  • She wanted to learn English. 她那時想學英文。

主詞或時間變了，主要動詞變成 want、wants 或 wanted。to learn 保持同樣的形式，在這三句裡都回答「想做什麼？」

不定詞也有不帶 to 的形式。例如 I can help.（我能幫忙），can 後面直接接原形 help。

什麼是介系詞的 to？

介系詞用來連接後面的對象。例如 send it to Amy 裡，to Amy 指寄送的對象。look forward to 裡，to 引出期待的事，像 the visit（這次拜訪）或 visiting your office（拜訪你的辦公室）。V-ing 就是像 visiting、working 這樣的動詞形式，在這裡把動作當成一件事來說。

快速判斷｜先找 to 前面的搭配

want to work

不定詞 + 原形 想做、決定做、需要做，或補充「為了做什麼」。

look forward to working

介系詞 + V-ing 把「做某事」當成一件事，放進原本可接名詞的搭配。

用名詞試看看。 look forward to the meeting 成立，所以也能說 look forward to meeting you。試的時候要保留同一個句型和意思。像 I want a break 直接接名詞，I want to take a break 才用不定詞。

to + 原形｜不定詞

常見動詞
want / plan / hope / decide / need / agree / promise / offer / refuse / manage + to V。We decided to postpone the meeting. 我們決定延期。
動詞 + 人 + to V
ask / tell / remind / allow / encourage / require + 人 + to V。Please remind Alex to reply. 請提醒 Alex 回覆。
形容詞 + to V
ready to start 準備好開始、likely to increase 可能增加、happy to help 樂意幫忙。
目的
I called to confirm the reservation. 我打電話是為了確認訂位。可用 in order to confirm 驗證目的用法。
名詞 + to V
a decision to expand 擴張的決定、a chance to learn 學習的機會。

原形也包括 be 和 have。例如 to be delivered（被送達）、to be working（正在工作）、to have finished（已經完成）。這三組中緊接 to 的都是原形。

to + V-ing｜介系詞搭配

同一個片語可以接名詞。後面要放動作時，把動作寫成 V-ing。

look forward to 期待
look forward to the visit ／ look forward to visiting your office
be / get used to 習慣
be used to the schedule ／ get used to working remotely
object to / be opposed to 反對
object to the change ／ object to changing the schedule
be committed to 致力於
be committed to quality ／ be committed to improving quality
contribute to / lead to 促成
contribute to the project ／ contribute to reducing costs。lead to higher sales ／ lead to losing customers。
devote time to 把時間投入
devote time to the project ／ devote time to training new staff
in addition to 除了……還
in addition to the fee ／ in addition to paying the fee
with a view to 以……為目的
with a view to expansion ／ with a view to expanding overseas。這是一個固定片語。
the key to / an approach to
the key to success ／ the key to reducing errors。an approach to hiring new staff 指一種徵才方法。

易錯句型｜三種 used to

used to + 原形
I used to work nights. 我以前常上夜班，通常暗示現在情況改變。
be / get used to + V-ing
I'm used to working nights. 我習慣上夜班。I'm getting used to working nights. 我正在適應上夜班。
be used to + 原形
This app is used to track orders. 這個 app 被用來追蹤訂單。is used 是被動，to track 說明用途。

再看兩個常考對比

  • stop to check 停下原本的事，為了查看。stop checking 停止查看這個動作。
  • remember to send it 記得要寄。remember sending it 記得自己寄過。
  • enjoy reading、avoid making mistakes、consider changing the plan 直接接 V-ing。背動詞時把句型一起背。

小測驗｜原形還是 V-ing？

先判斷整個片語的意思，再展開答案。

We plan to ___ the order today. （ship / shipping）

ship。plan to + 原形 表示計畫做某事。

I look forward to ___ from you. （hear / hearing）

hearing。look forward to 可以接名詞，如 your reply。

I used to ___ the bus to work. （take / taking）

take。used to + 原形 表示過去的習慣。

I'm getting used to ___ the new software. （use / using）

using。get used to 指漸漸習慣，to 是介系詞。

This tool is used to ___ invoices. （check / checking）

check。工具「被用來」檢查發票，to check 說明用途。

We are committed to ___ errors. （reduce / reducing）

reducing。be committed to 表「致力於」，其後可接名詞或 V-ing。

The manager called to ___ the reservation. （confirm / confirming）

confirm。用不定詞說明打電話的目的。

Please remember to ___ the file before noon. （send / sending）

send。remember to send 是記得要寄，動作還沒做。

動詞句型

及物與不及物動詞

先認識「直接受詞」，再看動詞後面要直接接對象，還是用介系詞帶出對象。

什麼是直接受詞？常見搭配兩種用法解題方法小測驗

什麼是直接受詞？

直接受詞（direct object）是動詞直接接的對象。 在 Mia checked the invoice. 裡，Mia 是主詞，checked 是動詞。問「Mia 檢查了什麼？」答案 the invoice 就是直接受詞。

及物用法｜動詞 + 直接受詞

The driver reached the airport. 司機抵達了機場。reach 在這裡直接接地點 the airport。

不及物用法｜動詞本身沒有直接受詞

The driver arrived at the airport. 司機抵達了機場。at the airport 整組說明抵達的地點，the airport 接在介系詞 at 後面。

「及物」的「物」可以是人、事或物。例如 contact Mia（聯絡 Mia）裡的受詞就是人。字典常用 [T] 標示及物、[I] 標示不及物。有些動詞會標示兩種。

常見搭配｜把動詞和後面的結構一起背

抵達
reach the airport ／ arrive at the airport。城市或國家用 arrive in Taipei、arrive in Taiwan。
討論
discuss the plan ／ talk about the plan。
進入
enter the meeting room ／ go into the meeting room。
參加
attend the workshop 出席工作坊，participate in the workshop 參與工作坊。
聯絡
contact the supplier ／ get in touch with the supplier。
回覆
answer the email ／ reply to the email。

不及物動詞也可以單獨出現。The guests arrived. 是完整句子。要補充時間可以寫 arrived at noon，要補充地點則寫 arrived at the hotel。

同一個動詞可以有兩種用法

increase
The store increased its prices. 店家調高了價格，its prices 是受詞。
Prices increased last month. 價格上個月上漲了，last month 說明時間。
open
Mia opened the door. Mia 打開了門。
The door opened slowly. 門慢慢打開了。
start
The host started the meeting at noon. 主持人在中午讓會議開始。
The meeting started at noon. 會議在中午開始。

另有兩個常考的「上升／提高」。Sales rose. 是銷售額上升。They raised prices. 是他們提高了價格。背成 rise 自己上升、raise + 對象 把某物提高。

解題方法｜先找動詞，再看後面的字

  1. 找對象。 She explained the rule to us. 裡，解釋的內容 the rule 是直接受詞，to us 補充說給誰聽。及物句也可以同時有介系詞片語。
  2. 分辨時間、地點和描述。 She left last night. 的 last night 說明何時離開。She became a manager. 的 a manager 描述主詞的新身分，文法上叫「主詞補語」。兩處的名詞片語都各有自己的功能。
  3. 用常見被動句型交叉檢查。 把直接受詞放到句首，寫成 The supplier shipped the order. → The order was shipped.。The order arrived. 裡，the order 本來就是主詞，直接用 arrived。

小測驗｜動詞後面接什麼？

先找出直接受詞或介系詞片語，再展開解析。

The delivery truck ___ the warehouse at noon. （arrived / reached）

reached。地點直接接在動詞後。另一個說法是 arrived at the warehouse。

We ___ the proposal during the meeting. （discussed / talked）

discussed。discuss the proposal 直接接提案。若用 talked，搭配是 talked about the proposal。

Rita ___ in a workshop yesterday. （attended / participated）

participated。記 participate in a workshop 和 attend a workshop。

We sent a reminder to the supplier. 哪個是直接受詞？

a reminder。問「寄了什麼？」答案是提醒通知。to the supplier 說明收件人。

Prices increased last month. 這裡的 increased 是及物還是不及物？

不及物。last month 說明時間。寫成 The store increased its prices. 時，its prices 才是直接受詞。

Please ___ to the customer's email by noon. （answer / reply）

reply。reply to the email 有介系詞 to，answer the email 則直接接對象。

The company ___ prices by 5 percent. （rose / raised）

raised。prices 是被提高的對象。也可以說 Prices rose by 5 percent.，此時價格是主詞。

The shipment ___ yesterday. （arrived / was arrived）

arrived。arrive 是不及物用法，表示貨物到了。若要表達「貨物被寄出」，可寫 The shipment was sent.。
